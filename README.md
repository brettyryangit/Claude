# Trading Journal

A written record of every trade — the setup, the plan, the execution, and the
honest post-mortem. The point is not to record P&L. The point is to make
patterns in my own behaviour visible enough that I cannot argue with them.

## How it works

1. **Before entry** — copy `trades/TEMPLATE.md` to `trades/NNNN.md` (next number),
   fill in everything above the `while open` section. If a field can't be filled
   in, the trade isn't ready.
2. **While open** — log adjustments as they happen, with the reason at the time.
   No retroactive reasons.
3. **After close** — fill in the exit, the result, and the six questions from
   [`CHECKLIST.md`](CHECKLIST.md). Answer them about *this* trade, honestly.
4. **Weekly** — reread the last week of entries. Count the yeses.

## Rules

- Every trade gets an entry. Winners, losers, scratches, and the ones I'm
  embarrassed about — especially those.
- The plan is written **before** the money is on. A plan written after entry is
  a story, not a plan.
- Risk per trade is fixed and written down. Changing it mid-trade gets logged as
  a rule break, not as "adapting".
- A loss taken according to plan is a good trade. A win taken by breaking the
  plan is a bad trade.
