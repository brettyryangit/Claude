## Note

First trade with an exit/management rule stated **before** it was needed —
0001 and 0002 both had "not written before entry" here. The rule was: *move
to break-even if the next candle makes a new low.* It made one, and the stop
moved exactly as planned.

Worth being precise about Checklist Q6 here rather than flattening it: Q6 is
about moving a stop to break-even **for the sole reason that you can't now
lose** — a fear reaction with no structural basis. This one had the condition
written down before entry and executed on the condition being met, not on a
feeling once in profit. That's a different thing from Q6, and the distinction
only holds because the rule was on paper first. If future BE moves show up
*without* a rule written beforehand, that's the ones to flag.
